Use case: production sprite sheet for a 2D grid warehouse game. Produce an isolated game sprite atlas, NOT a scene or concept screenshot. Style reference: the attached image "warehouse-concept-v3.png". Closely match its charming round yellow robots, crisp dark outlines, clean simplified painted surfaces, soft bevel shading, deep blue metal, teal accents, warm kraft boxes. Polished cartoon-illustrated game sprites, NOT realistic materials, no gritty texture.

FORMAT RULES (strict):
1. Fully transparent background: true alpha-channel transparency (RGBA PNG). Absolutely no checkerboard pattern, no solid backdrop color anywhere.
2. Each object's soft contact shadow is baked into its own sprite as semi-transparent dark pixels hugging its base. Shadows are COMPACT: at most 15% larger than the object's base, never a wide smeared blob.
3. Fixed near-top-down orthographic camera with slightly visible front faces, identical for every object. Light always from the upper left. Never rotate finished sprites in the image plane; redraw each facing under the fixed camera and light.
4. One consistent world scale: one grid cell equals the same square footprint in every slot.
5. Fill exactly the slots described below, each exactly once, complete and uncropped, centered in its slot, generous equal padding. Never invent extra objects to fill empty space; never duplicate a slot.
6. Exactly 4 columns by 3 rows, 12 slots. High resolution.
7. No labels, no text, no letters, no numbers, no UI panels, no borders, no grid lines, no slot dividers, no watermark.

ATLAS A: ROBOT, 12 slots.

Robot design identical to the reference: compact round yellow body, charcoal round cargo lid on top, dark lower bumper with two discreet dark side wheels, a short arc of tiny glowing blue face lights on the front. Dome-shaped, armless, legless, friendly. The robot's ground footprint is one grid cell: the body fills about 80% of the cell width so it never appears to touch the cell borders. Low dome profile.

CONSISTENCY RULE (critical): all 12 robots are the SAME model, SAME size, SAME camera, SAME light — only the facing and the cargo state change. Row 1, 2 and 3 must look like three frames of the same robot.

Row 1 — empty robot, four facings:
(1,1) facing SOUTH: front toward the viewer, blue face lights fully visible.
(1,2) facing NORTH: rear toward the viewer, plain yellow back arc with a small vent detail, no face lights visible.
(1,3) facing EAST: front toward the right edge, face lights along the right rim.
(1,4) facing WEST: front toward the left edge, face lights along the left rim.

Row 2 — idle blink frames: exact copies of row 1, same order SOUTH, NORTH, EAST, WEST, identical in every way except the blue face lights are drawn as thin closed dark lash lines instead of glowing dots. The north-facing rear has no face, so (2,2) is identical to (1,2).

Row 3 — robot carrying ONE plain kraft carton (no icon, just cardboard and tape) sitting flat and centered on its cargo lid: same four facings, same order SOUTH, NORTH, EAST, WEST. The carton is about 60% of the lid width and must not cover the face lights on the south-facing sprite. Identical robot scale and slot placement as row 1.
