Use case: production sprite sheet for a 2D grid warehouse game. Produce an isolated game sprite atlas, NOT a scene or concept screenshot. Style reference: the attached image "warehouse-concept-v3.png". Closely match its crisp dark outlines, clean simplified painted surfaces, soft bevel shading, deep blue metal shelves, teal chargers, warm kraft boxes, white-cab deep-blue-bed trucks, yellow-black hazard markings. Polished cartoon-illustrated game sprites, NOT realistic materials, no gritty texture.

FORMAT RULES (strict):
1. Fully transparent background: true alpha-channel transparency (RGBA PNG). Absolutely no checkerboard pattern, no solid backdrop color anywhere.
2. Each object's soft contact shadow is baked into its own sprite as semi-transparent dark pixels hugging its base. Shadows are COMPACT: at most 15% larger than the object's base.
3. Fixed near-top-down orthographic camera with slightly visible front faces, identical for every object. Light always from the upper left. Never rotate finished sprites in the image plane; redraw each facing under the fixed camera and light.
4. AXIS-ALIGNMENT (critical): every square or rectangular object (shelf, charger, dock pad, floor marking, carton) sits AXIS-ALIGNED with the grid — its front edge runs perfectly horizontal and its side edges perfectly vertical in the image. NEVER draw a square footprint rotated into a diamond, and never slightly tilted or skewed.
5. One consistent world scale: the storage shelf, the charging station and the truck are all exactly ONE grid cell wide; one grid cell equals the same square footprint in every slot.
6. Fill exactly the slots described below, each exactly once, complete and uncropped, centered in its slot, generous equal padding. Never invent extra objects; never duplicate a slot.
7. Exactly 5 columns by 3 rows, 15 slots. High resolution.
8. No labels, no text, no letters, no numbers, no UI panels, no borders, no grid lines, no slot dividers, no watermark.

ATLAS B: STORAGE AND LOGISTICS, 15 slots.

Row 1 — goods cartons: five standalone boxes of IDENTICAL geometry and size (ground footprint one grid cell, suitable to be carried by a small robot, stacked on shelves or loaded into truck beds). Each icon is printed LARGE on the TOP face and repeated on the front face — the top face is what players see most:
(1,1) water carton: blue droplet symbol.
(1,2) battery carton: green lightning symbol.
(1,3) chip carton: purple microchip symbol.
(1,4) plain carton: no symbol, just kraft cardboard and packing tape.
(1,5) crushed carton: same kraft carton flattened, torn open and crumpled, clearly destroyed.

Row 2 — storage, charging and transport:
(2,1) storage shelf, EMPTY: a single-layer low platform in the reference's deep blue metal style, ground footprint one cell, perfectly SQUARE and AXIS-ALIGNED (front rail perfectly horizontal, side rails perfectly vertical — NOT rotated, NOT tilted, NOT diamond-oriented), LOW height (about one third of a cell), flat top surface with low guide rails around the edges, clearly meant to hold boxes in a SINGLE layer. NOT a tall multi-tier rack, no upper level.
(2,2) charging station, idle: teal pillar-and-pad charger matching the reference, small dark unlit screen, hazard-striped base pad.
(2,3) charging station, working: identical to (2,2) but screen and top lamp glowing bright teal with a lit lightning symbol.
(2,4) truck facing SOUTH, empty: white cab, deep blue cargo bed.
(2,5) truck facing NORTH, empty: rear of the cargo bed toward the viewer, cab at the far side.

TRUCK PROPORTION RULE (critical): a truck's ground footprint is ONE cell wide and TWO cells long. Even with the camera foreshortening, the truck must read clearly LONGER than wide — visual aspect about 1 : 1.6, a long vehicle. The cab takes about one third of the length and the open cargo bed about two thirds, bed floor visible from above. A squat, square-looking truck is WRONG.

Row 3 — loading dock pads and floor marking, all painted FLAT on the ground:
(3,1) vertical dock pad, empty: painted concrete pad one cell wide and two cells long, long axis up-down, bordered by yellow-black hazard stripes, plain dashed outline marking it free.
(3,2) vertical pad, occupied: same pad, edge indicator lamps glowing orange-red to show a vehicle is docked.
(3,3) horizontal pad, empty: same pad, long axis left-right.
(3,4) horizontal pad, occupied: edge indicator lamps glowing orange-red.
(3,5) short yellow-black hazard stripe strip painted flat on the floor, one cell long, as floor marking.

PAD PROPORTION RULE (critical): like the truck, each pad's ground footprint is one cell wide and two cells long; it must read clearly longer than wide (about 1 : 1.6).

All 15 items must share one style, one camera, one light.
